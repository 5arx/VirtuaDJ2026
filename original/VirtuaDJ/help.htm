Main

This turntable simulation works just like the real thing. To play a record:

 Turn the power on

 Start the turntable

 Raise the arm by using the lever

 Move the arm over the record by dragging the headshell

 Lower the arm and …

 DISCO!

You may also change the speed and pitch of the record by using the 33 1/3 and 45 rpm buttons and the slider to the right of the deck.

Virtua DJ Mode

The turntables work the same, but this time you can play two records at once. Try it!
